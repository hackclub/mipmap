# Hack Club Mipmap Docs

So! You want to make a game without an engine, but you're not quite sure where to start. That's totally fine!

In the onboarding tutorial, you learned about the three tiers. Tier Quebec is all about 2D games without an engine, and yes, things like the JS canvas and Pygame count here. Tier Sierra is the trickiest: something made in Vulkan, on bare metal, or a 3D renderer made without a library. Tier Romeo is anything in the middle.

Mips payout rates are anywhere between 15 and 25 Mips per hour. This depends on a bunch of things, such as the tier, your Snapshot bonus, other bonuses, and more!

If you're wanting to do something 3D, you'll probably want to look into a low level GPU graphics library. These let you tell the GPU exactly what you want to be doing. For a primer on rendering beyond this, check out the video linked on this page!

For your game, you'll also need all sorts of other miscellaneous things. This would include sound, input, interfacing with the OS, and a bunch more. However, none of these things are too tricky individually, so they're not taught directly within Mipmap. Your search engine or LLM of choice can help you understand these.

Also, Mipmap can't possibly teach every single graphics library to ever exist. That's why the video covers concepts common to all of them, but you'll also find links to a bunch of resources for common libraries on this page.

There's also a little quiz picker thing if you're not sure which to use.

If you're confused about anything at all, [email](mailto:brendan@hackclub.com) me or ask in [#mipmap](https://hackclub.enterprise.slack.com/archives/C0C2UMX4S9E). We're here to help and make sure you make something you're truly proud of!
